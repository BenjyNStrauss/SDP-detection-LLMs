Below are some related insights you might find useful:

- Many of these core Camel classes leverage the Factory and Strategy families to stay extensible and configurable at runtime.
- Utility classes like `Strings` and `JavadocHelper` often resemble Facade patterns, masking complexity behind simple static methods.
- Interfaces such as `AggregationStrategy` double as Strategy definitions and employ default (Template Method) hooks for common lifecycle events.
- For test code, Builder and Factory Method patterns help construct and wire test scenarios in a fluent, expressive manner.
- The presence of `Null Object` and `Holder` idioms shows Camel’s commitment to avoiding null checks and fostering immutable, self-descriptive types.
