>## Summary Insights

**Most Common Patterns Across Files:**
1. **Template Method Pattern** - Present in 8/10 files
2. **Strategy Pattern** - Present in 8/10 files  
3. **Adapter Pattern** - Present in 6/10 files
4. **Dependency Injection Pattern** - Present in 4/10 files
5. **Builder Pattern** - Present in 6/10 files

**Confidence Assessment Methodology:**
- **Detection Confidence**: Based on clear structural evidence and pattern characteristics
- **Correctness**: Accounts for proper implementation and adherence to pattern intent

**Key Observations:**
- High prevalence of behavioral patterns (Template Method, Strategy)
- Strong use of structural patterns (Adapter, Facade) for API compatibility
- Maven's architecture heavily relies on dependency injection and modular design
- Consistent use of immutability patterns for thread safety and reliability
