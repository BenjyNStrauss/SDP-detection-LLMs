>## Analysis Summary

The Apache PDFBox codebase demonstrates sophisticated use of design patterns:

- **Most Common Patterns**: Factory Method, Strategy, Template Method appear frequently
- **Performance-Oriented**: Flyweight (COSInteger) and Cache patterns for optimization  
- **Flexibility-Oriented**: Strategy and Factory patterns for extensible architectures
- **Structural Patterns**: Adapter, Facade, and Wrapper patterns for clean interfaces
- **Behavioral Patterns**: Visitor, Template Method for algorithm variations

The high confidence scores reflect clear pattern implementations, while correctness percentages account for partial implementations or hybrid approaches common in production code.
