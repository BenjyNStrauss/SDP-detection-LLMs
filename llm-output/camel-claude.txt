>## Summary

The most commonly implemented patterns across these Apache Camel files are:

1. **Factory Pattern** (40% of files) - Extensive use for creating various components
2. **Strategy Pattern** (60% of files) - Flexible algorithm selection and behavior variation
3. **Template Method** (70% of files) - Consistent processing patterns with customizable steps
4. **Utility/Helper** (30% of files) - Static utility methods for common operations
5. **Chain of Responsibility** (50% of files) - Sequential processing and fallback mechanisms

These patterns reflect Apache Camel's architecture focusing on flexibility, extensibility, and consistent processing patterns for enterprise integration.
